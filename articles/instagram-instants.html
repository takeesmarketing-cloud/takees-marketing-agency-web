Instagram just dropped something really exciting, and if you haven’t heard about it yet, you’re in the right place. Instagram Instants is a brand-new feature that brings back the joy of sharing raw, real moments with your friends without the pressure of filters, edits, or perfect lighting. Think of it as Instagram going back to its roots, but in a completely new way.

What are Instagram Instants?

Instagram Instant is a new way to share photos in the moment with your close friends or mutual followers. Unlike your regular Instagram posts or stories, Instants are casual, unedited snapshots of your life as it happens. Once your friend views an Instant, it disappears, just like that.

Instagram officially launched this feature globally on May 13, 2026, with a standalone Instants app also rolling out in select countries like Spain and Italy. The idea is simple: stop overthinking your posts and just share the moment.

How Do Instagram Instants Work?

Using Instagram Instant is super simple. Here’s how you can get started right now:

  1. Open your Instagram app and go to your inbox(DMs).
  2. Look for the mini pile of photos icon at the bottom right corner of your inbox.
  3. Tap it to open the Instant camera and take a photo in real time.
  4. Add a text caption if you want(no other edits allowed).
  5. Choose to share it with your Close Friends or friends
  6. That’s it- your instant is sent!

Note: You cannot upload photos from your gallery. Instants only works with real-time photos taken directly in the app. This is what makes it so authentic and spontaneous.

Key Features of Instagram Instants You Should Know

  • No edits allowed
  • Disappears after viewing
  • No screenshots
  • Private archives
  • Recap to stories

Instagram Instants vs Snapchat: Is it Really That Different?

Honestly? The comparison is hard to miss. Instagram Instants looks and feels a lot like the original Snapchat, disappearing photos, no edits, and real-time only sharing. Many people online have pointed this out, too.

Why Instagram Instants Still Makes Sense in 2026

Even though Snapchat pioneered this format, Instagram Instants has a huge advantage, it lives inside an app that billions of people already use every single day. You don’t need to switch apps or convince your friends to download something new. Your whole network is already there.

Plus, the integration with Instagram’s existing features, like Stories, Close Friends, and your DM inbox, makes Instants feel like a natural extension of how you already use the platform.

How Instagram Instants is different from Stories?

Instagram Stories are broadcast; they go out to all your followers (or a selected group) and stay up for 24 hours. Instagram Instants, on the other hand, are more personal and private. They go only to people you specifically choose, and they disappear the moment the person views them. It’s the difference between posting for an audience and sharing a moment with a friend.

Is Instagram Instants Safe for Teens?

Yes, Instagram has built in strong safety measures for younger users. For teen accounts, Instants automatically includes:

  • Shared time limits: Time on Instants counts toward the daily Instagram usage limit.
  • Sleep Mode: Notifications are muted between 10 PM and 7 AM by default.
  • Parent notifications: Parents are notified the first time their teen downloads the Instants app.
  • Block, mute, and restrict tools: All of Instagram’s existing safety features work on Instants too.

 How to Disable Instagram Instants if you don’t want it

Not everyone is a fan of the new feature, and that’s okay! Instagram has made it easy to opt out. If you’d rather not receive Instants from others, simply hold down the pile of Instants in your inbox and swipe right to temporarily stop receiving them. You can also block or mute specific people from sending you Instants.

What Does Instagram Instants Mean for Brands and Content Creators?

A New Door for Authentic Brand Storytelling

If you’re a brand or creator, Instagram Instants opens up a fresh way to connect with your most loyal fans. Sharing behind-the-scenes moments, sneak peeks, or just everyday life at your office without the pressure of making it “Insta-perfect” can actually build more trust and loyalty than a polished carousel ever could.

Use Instants to Humanize Your Brand

Audiences in 2026 crave realness. They want to see the human side of the brands they follow. Instagram Instants is the perfect tool to show that. Whether it’s a quick photo from a team lunch, a raw product shot, or a candid moment from your workspace, these small, authentic touches go a long way.

How to Make the Most of Instagram Instants for Your Business

Here are some practical tips to use Instagram Instants as part of your social media marketing:

  • Share behind-the-scenes moments from product launches or events.
  • Send exclusive sneak peeks to your Close Friends list (your VIP customers or top fans).
  • Use the Recap to Stories feature to compile your best Instants into a Story highlight.
  • Respond to customer DMs with an Instant for a more personal, human touch.
  • Run flash campaigns- “Send us an Instant of you using our product!”, for user-generated content.
