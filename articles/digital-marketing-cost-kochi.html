Ask three agencies in Kochi for a digital marketing quote, and you may get three very different numbers. Many agencies also hide their prices behind a “contact us” button. That makes it hard to plan a budget.

Quick answer. Digital marketing cost in Kochi is usually around ₹10,000 to ₹30,000 a month for a small business, ₹30,000 to ₹80,000 for a growing business, and ₹80,000 or more for larger brands. These are agency fees only. Money you spend on Google or Meta ads is usually paid on top. All figures in this guide are estimates, not fixed rates.

In this guide you will see what each service costs, which costs agencies often leave out of a quote, and what budget suits different types of Kochi businesses. You will also learn the warning signs that a cheap offer will cost you more later.

How Much Each Digital Marketing Service Costs in Kochi

Each service is priced in its own way. Some are monthly, some are one-time, and some depend on how much you spend on ads. Here is what changes the price for each one.

How much does SEO cost in Kochi?

SEO usually costs ₹15,000 to ₹50,000 a month in Kochi. Local SEO sits at the lower end. It covers your Google Business Profile, map rankings and a few service pages. Wider SEO for all of Kerala, India or the GCC costs more because you compete with more websites. A dental clinic in Kakkanad that wants local patients needs far less work than an online store selling across India. Learn more about our SEO services.

How much does social media marketing cost?

Social media management usually costs ₹10,000 to ₹50,000 a month. The price depends mostly on how many posts and reels you get each month and how many platforms are covered. Posting in both Malayalam and English takes more work than posting in one language. Ask whether the price includes reels, captions, replying to comments and paid boosting. See how social media marketing can help you build a brand.

How much does Google Ads cost?

Google Ads has two separate costs. The first is the agency fee for setting up and running your campaigns. Kochi agencies often charge a setup fee of around ₹10,000 to ₹30,000 and then 10% to 20% of your ad spend each month. The second is your ad budget, which you pay to Google. Google charges you each time someone clicks your ad. This is called cost per click, or CPC. Competitive searches like “flats in Kochi” cost more per click than quieter ones.

How much do Meta Ads cost?

Meta Ads run on Facebook and Instagram. They follow the same two-part cost as Google Ads: an agency fee plus your ad budget paid to Meta. Many Kochi businesses use lead form ads or ads that open a WhatsApp chat. These work well for real estate, clinics, events and retail offers. A small test budget helps you learn what works before you spend more.

How much does website design cost in Kochi?

A website is a one-time cost, with small yearly costs after that. A simple business website may cost around ₹15,000 to ₹40,000. A larger custom website or an online store can cost ₹1,00,000 or more. You also pay every year for a domain name, hosting and maintenance. Ask who owns the website and its login details once it is built. Explore our website development service.

How much does branding cost?

Branding can mean just a logo or a full brand identity. A logo alone costs less. A full identity includes colours, fonts, a brand voice and a guideline document your team can follow. That takes more time, so it costs more. New businesses and brands planning a rebrand gain the most from a full package. See our branding and identity design work.

How much does video and reel production cost?

Video is usually priced per reel or per shoot day. A simple reel shot on a phone costs much less than a commercial with a crew, lights, actors and editing. Shooting at more than one location also adds cost. Many Kochi brands plan one shoot day to create several reels at once, which lowers the cost per video. Learn about our video production service.

What Agency Fees Include and What You Pay Separately

Most confusion about digital marketing costs comes from mixing up three different costs. Knowing them helps you compare quotes fairly.

  1. Agency fee. This is what you pay the agency for planning, creating and managing your marketing.
  2. Ad budget. This is the money that goes to Google or Meta to show your ads. The agency does not keep it.
  3. Other costs. These include GST, domain and hosting, paid software tools, stock photos and printing.

Is the ad budget included in the agency fee?

Usually not. Most agencies charge a fee for managing your ads, and you pay the ad platform separately for the ads themselves. Some agencies offer one combined price, so always ask for both numbers to be shown on their own lines. This way you know exactly how much reaches your customers and how much goes to the agency.

How GST adds to your total

GST is usually charged at 18% on digital marketing services in India. Google also charges GST on ad purchases. Its help page lists an 18% rate for India, split into central and state GST when your billing state matches Google’s. Meta ad invoices can be handled differently depending on which Meta company bills you, so check your invoice and ask your accountant.

Here is a simple example of one month’s spend. The numbers are only for illustration.

A quote that says “₹20,000 a month” can turn into almost ₹60,000 once ads and tax are added. If your business is registered for GST, you can usually claim this GST back as input tax credit. Confirm this with your accountant.

What Makes Digital Marketing Cost More or Less

Two Kochi businesses can pay very different amounts for the same service. These five factors explain why.

  • How competitive your industry is. A search like “builders in Kochi” has many businesses fighting for it. Ranking or advertising there needs more work and a bigger ad budget than “bakery in Edappally”.
  • How many services you need. One platform costs less than SEO, social media and ads together. Each extra channel adds planning, content and reporting.
  • Where your customers are. Targeting one area of Kochi is cheaper than targeting all of Kerala. Reaching NRI customers in the GCC adds more cost again.
  • Where you are starting from. A business with no website or reviews needs setup work first. An established brand can often start campaigns straight away.
  • How much content you need. Ten reels a month cost more than four. Professional shoots cost more than phone videos.

When you get a quote, ask which of these factors shaped the price. A good agency can explain it clearly.

Warning Signs That Cheap Digital Marketing Will Cost More

A low price is not always a good deal. Watch for these signs before you sign.

  • Guaranteed first-page rankings. No one controls Google, so this promise often leads to risky shortcuts.
  • No regular reports. Without reports you cannot see where your money went.
  • Ad accounts in the agency’s name. If you leave, you may lose your ad history and data. Your accounts should be in your business name.
  • Fake followers or likes. They make numbers look good but bring no real customers.
  • Vague deliverables. “Social media management” means little without a post count.
  • Long lock-in contracts. Being stuck for a year with poor results is expensive.

Frequently Asked Questions About Digital Marketing Costs in Kochi

What is the minimum budget to start digital marketing in Kochi?

A small business can often start with around ₹10,000 to ₹15,000 a month in agency fees for basics like a Google Business Profile and simple social media. If you want to run ads, plan a separate ad budget too. Starting small and growing with results is a sensible approach.

Is GST charged on digital marketing services?

Yes. GST is usually charged at 18% on agency fees in India. Ad platforms such as Google also charge GST on ad spend. If your business is GST registered, you can usually claim it back as an input tax credit. Check the details with your accountant.

Is the Google Ads budget included in agency fees?

Usually not. The agency fee pays for managing your campaigns. The ad budget is paid to Google for clicks on your ads. Always ask for both amounts to be shown separately in your quote.

How long does it take to see results?

Ads can bring enquiries within days of going live. SEO and organic social media usually take a few months to build up. Read more about how long digital marketing takes to show results.

Should a small business start with SEO or ads?

If you need enquiries quickly, start with ads. If you want lower costs over time, start SEO early because it builds slowly. Many Kochi businesses run a small ad campaign while their SEO grows.
