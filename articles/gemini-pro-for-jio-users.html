How to Add or Claim Gemini Pro for All Jio Users

What is the Offer

Google has partnered with Reliance Jio to provide its premium AI service, Google AI Pro (Gemini 2.5 Pro), to eligible Jio users for free for 18 months.

The offer includes access to Gemini’s advanced model, 2 TB cloud storage, image and video generation tools, and expanded research and writing assistance via Google’s AI tools.

Who’s Eligible

According to Jio’s official page:

  • Only Jio users with an Unlimited 5G plan of ₹ 349 or more (prepaid or postpaid) can avail this.
  • Initially, the offer is for users aged 18 to 25 years.
  • You must have a Jio SIM and the plan must be active during the subscription period.

Later, the offer may expand beyond that age group.

Key Benefits

If you are eligible, you get:

  • Access to Gemini 2.5 Pro model via the Gemini app, 2 TB cloud storage (Drive, Photos, Gmail).
  • Image and video generation tools.
  • Enhanced AI features within Google Workspace (Gmail, Docs, etc.)
  • Research and writing assistance via NotebookLM with higher limits.

Steps to Claim Gemini Pro via MyJio

  1. Open MyJio app
    Make sure your Jio account is active and you have the required eligible plan.
  2. Look for the Banner or Offer
    On the home screen of MyJio, you should see a “Google Gemini Offer” banner or something similar.
  3. Tap or Claim Offer
    Click the banner. It may ask you to register your interest or tap “Get Offer.”
  4. Connect Your Gmail or Google Account
    You will have to link your Google account, to which will be used with the AI Pro subscription.
  5. Keep Your Plan Active
    Maintain your eligible 5G unlimited plan (₹349 or above) during the entire subscription duration (18 months) to retain benefits.
  6. Start Using Gemini Pro Features
    Once claimed successfully, you can access Gemini Pro features through the Gemini app or Google AI tools on your linked Google account.

Things to Watch Out

  • If your age is above 25, the rollout might take more time. You may need to register your interest and wait for full rollout.
  • Keep your plan active continuously. If you switch or cancel your Jio plan, access might be lost.
  • Non-Jio users can become eligible by porting to Jio or getting a new Jio SIM with the required plan.
  • Make sure your Google account is valid and follow all registration steps correctly.

Why This is Useful

  • Get high-end AI access without paying extra, with pro-level tools for writing, image or video generation, and research.
  • Great value, as the subscription is worth around ₹35,100 and is free under this offer.
  • Especially useful for students, creators, and developers who can use Gemini’s pro features in everyday work with Google’s productivity apps.

Final Thoughts

This collaboration between Google and Jio is a game-changer for users in India. With Gemini Pro now available for free, even everyday users can explore advanced AI features without spending extra money. Whether you’re into content creation, studying, or exploring AI tools for productivity, Gemini Pro can make your workflow faster and more creative. It’s a smart way to experience premium AI before it becomes a global standard.

If you’re a Jio user, don’t miss this offer. Claim it now through the MyJio app and unlock a powerful AI assistant designed to make your digital life easier.
