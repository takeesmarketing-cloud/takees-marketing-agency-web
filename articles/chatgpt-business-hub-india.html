Picture a patient in Kannur who opens ChatGPT and types, “Which skin clinic in Kannur is good for acne treatment?” More customers now ask AI tools questions like this instead of scrolling through Google results.

On 10 November 2026, OpenAI launched ChatGPT Business Hub in beta. It lets businesses claim their profile inside ChatGPT and see how people find them there. For Indian businesses, though, the answer to “Can I use it?” is not a simple yes.

In this guide, you’ll learn what ChatGPT Business Hub is, which Indian businesses can use it today, how to claim a listing, and what to do if you can’t claim yet.

Quick answer: Partly. Indian online businesses with a website, such as e-commerce stores, agencies and software companies, may be able to claim a profile. Local businesses like clinics, shops and restaurants can’t claim yet, because local claiming currently covers only existing US listings. Business agents are US-only for now.

What Is ChatGPT Business Hub?

ChatGPT Business Hub is like Google Business Profile, but for ChatGPT. It’s a free dashboard where a business owner can claim their business, fix the details ChatGPT shows about it, and see how people interact with it.

It’s still in beta. That means it’s an early version, and OpenAI may change how it works in the coming months.

What Can Businesses Do With It?

Once your claim is approved and verified, you can:

  • Claim your profile so OpenAI knows you officially represent the business.
  • Correct your details, such as your website, contact information and business description.
  • Add product information, including prices and availability, through product feeds.
  • See Insights, which show how often ChatGPT mentions your website and how many people click through.
  • Create a business agent (US only for now). This is a chat assistant that answers customer questions about your business inside ChatGPT.

ChatGPT Business Hub vs ChatGPT Business

The names sound alike, but these are two different products.

It depends on whether your business sells mainly online or from a physical location. Here is what OpenAI’s current rules allow:

Think of two saree sellers. One runs an online saree store that ships across India. The other runs a saree shop in Kannur town. The online store may be able to claim a profile today. The shop in Kannur cannot, because ChatGPT currently lets local businesses claim only listings that already exist in the US.

There is early evidence that Indian online businesses can get through the process. One Indian agency owner reported that his draft profile was saved and that India appeared in the list of countries for the ID check. This is a single early report, not official confirmation from OpenAI.

OpenAI has said it plans to expand to more markets and business categories over time. It has not shared any dates.

How to Claim Your Business on ChatGPT

If your business qualifies, claiming takes four steps. Get these ready first, so you don’t get stuck halfway.

Keep these ready:

  • A ChatGPT account your business controls
  • Login access to where your domain is registered, such as GoDaddy, Hostinger or BigRock
  • A business email on your own domain (for example, hello@yourbrand.in)
  • Your business phone number
  • A government ID for the owner or authorised person

Step 1: Open Business Hub and Find Your Business

Sign in to ChatGPT and open Business Hub from the sidebar, or go to chatgpt.com/business-hub. Search for your business by name. Pick the listing that matches yours and confirm which ChatGPT account will manage it. OpenAI’s setup guide explains each screen.

Step 2: Verify Your Claim

Next, prove the business is yours. OpenAI offers methods such as business phone, business email, website domain or Shopify. You’ll see only the options that apply to your business.

The website domain method uses a DNS record. That’s a small line of text you add in your domain settings to show you own the website. Your web developer or hosting provider can add it in a few minutes. OpenAI then reviews your claim and emails you its decision.

Step 3: Pass the Persona ID Check

Approval is not the last step. OpenAI uses a company called Persona to check the identity of the person claiming. Until this check is done, you can’t publish changes, see Insights or create a Business Agent.

Step 4: Complete Your Profile

Now fill in your business details, such as your website, contact information, category and a short description. Write the description in plain words that match how customers describe what you do. Early users report a short character limit, so lead with your main service.

Before you accept, read OpenAI’s Businesses in ChatGPT Terms. Early users point out that a published profile can’t be deleted; only drafts can. If the terms worry you, ask a legal adviser.

What Can Businesses Learn From Business Hub Analytics?

Business Hub’s Insights page shows activity from the past 28 days. It has three main numbers:

  • Mentions: how often ChatGPT referred to your website in its answers.
  • Website clicks: how many people clicked through to your site from ChatGPT.
  • Discovery topics: the common themes in conversations where your business showed up.

Discovery topics are the most useful part. They work like free content ideas. Say an online saree store sees “Kerala kasavu saree price” as a topic. That’s a clear sign to write a page answering exactly that question.

Insights don’t show everything. You won’t see individual customer chats, competitor data or sales. OpenAI shows the numbers only when there is enough activity, so a new or small business may see little at first.

What Businesses Should Know About ChatGPT Ads and Sponsored Business Agents

ChatGPT has two ways a business can appear. The first is a free recommendation, where ChatGPT mentions you because you fit the question. The second is a paid placement. OpenAI says sponsored placements will be clearly labelled and kept separate from regular answers.

Businesses can sponsor their business agent and use it as the destination for ChatGPT ads. A customer could tap an ad and start chatting with that business inside ChatGPT.

For Indian businesses, this isn’t available yet, because business agents are currently US-only. If you already run Google Ads or Meta Ads, though, this is a channel worth watching.

How to Get Your Business Recommended by ChatGPT Even Without a Listing

ChatGPT can mention your business whether you claim a listing or not. It builds its answers from information across the web. So the work you do on your online presence today still counts. It’s the same groundwork that good SEO is built on.

These eight steps improve your chances:

  1. Keep your details the same everywhere. Use the exact same business name, address and phone number on your website, Google and directories. Mixed details confuse AI tools.
  2. Complete your Google Business Profile. Add your services, hours, photos and a clear description.
  3. Collect genuine Google reviews. Ask happy customers to mention the service they used, such as “root canal” or “wedding catering”.
  4. List on trusted Indian directories. Justdial, Sulekha and IndiaMART help, and so do category sites like Practo for clinics.
  5. Write clear service pages. Answer real customer questions on your website, including price ranges, locations served and timelines.
  6. Add schema markup. Schema is hidden code that tells search engines and AI tools facts about your business. Use the LocalBusiness or Organisation schema.
  7. Earn mentions on trusted websites. Local news sites, industry blogs and association pages all count.
  8. Check your robots.txt file. This file tells bots which pages they can read. Make sure it doesn’t block OpenAI’s search crawler, OAI-SearchBot.

These steps make it more likely ChatGPT will understand and mention your business. They can’t guarantee it, because ChatGPT decides on its own what to recommend.

What ChatGPT Business Hub Means for Businesses in Kerala and Bangalore

Here is what three typical businesses in Kannur, Kochi and Bangalore can do today.

A dental clinic in Kannur. A patient might ask ChatGPT, “Best dentist in Kannur for implants?” The clinic can’t claim a listing yet. It should focus on its Google Business Profile, Practo listing and detailed treatment pages, and on recent reviews that mention specific treatments.

A real estate builder in Kochi. A buyer might ask, “Trusted builders in Kochi for 3BHK flats?” If the builder gets most leads through its website, it may count as an online business, but that is not certain yet. The builder should check eligibility in Business Hub. Meanwhile, it should build a page for each project with location, price range and approval details.

A café in Bangalore. Someone might ask, “Are there quiet cafés in Indiranagar to work from?” The café can’t claim a listing yet. It should keep Google Maps, Zomato and Swiggy details correct and encourage reviews with photos.

Key Takeaways

  • ChatGPT Business Hub is a free tool, launched in beta on 10 November 2026, for managing how your business appears in ChatGPT.
  • Indian online businesses may be able to claim a profile. Indian local businesses can’t claim yet.
  • Claim approval is not the finish line. You must also pass a Persona ID check.
  • Claiming a listing doesn’t guarantee ChatGPT will recommend you.
  • Every business can start today with accurate listings, reviews, clear service pages and schema.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is ChatGPT Business Hub free?

Yes, it’s free at launch. OpenAI hasn’t said whether this will change.

2. Can Indian businesses claim a listing on ChatGPT?

Indian online businesses with a website may be able to. Local businesses such as shops, clinics and restaurants can’t claim yet.

3. What is a ChatGPT Business Agent?

It’s a chat assistant that answers customer questions about your business inside ChatGPT. For now, only eligible US e-commerce and online service businesses can create one.

4. Is ChatGPT Business Hub the same as ChatGPT Business?

No. Business Hub manages how your business appears in ChatGPT. ChatGPT Business is a paid plan for teams who use ChatGPT at work.

5. Does ChatGPT Business Hub affect Google rankings?

No, there’s no direct link. But accurate business details and helpful content support visibility on both. Google rankings also shift with updates like the Google November 2026 Core Update.

6. How long does business verification take?

OpenAI hasn’t published a timeline. It reviews each claim and emails you its decision. Early users reported finishing the claim form in about ten minutes.
