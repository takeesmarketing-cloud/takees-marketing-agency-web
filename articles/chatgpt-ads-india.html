India is ChatGPT’s second-largest market, with more than 100 million weekly users as of February 2026. Since 27 August 2026, many of them see something new below their answers. Ads.

ChatGPT ads in India are now open to almost any business. You can sign up yourself and start with a daily budget of ₹725. That low entry point is why so many owners and marketers are asking the same question. Should we try it?

The honest answer depends on your industry, your customers and your tracking. Some Indian businesses are a good fit. Others are blocked by OpenAI’s ad rules. This guide explains what ChatGPT ads are, what they really cost, who can use them, and how to run a first test without wasting money.

What Are ChatGPT Ads and How Do They Work?

ChatGPT ads are sponsored cards that appear below ChatGPT’s answer. They are clearly labelled and matched to what the user is talking about. Businesses buy them in OpenAI’s Ads Manager at ads.openai.com and pay for views, clicks or conversions.

Here is a simple example. A student in Kochi asks, “Which laptop under ₹60,000 is best for engineering?” ChatGPT gives its answer. Below it, an electronics store’s ad for student laptops may appear.

Advertisers guide this matching with “context hints”. These are short descriptions of the conversations where an ad fits. They are not exact keywords like in Google Ads. OpenAI says hints guide matching but do not guarantee your ad shows in a particular chat.

Two points matter for trust. Ads do not change ChatGPT’s answers, and advertisers cannot see anyone’s chats. They only receive totals such as views and clicks, according to OpenAI’s ads FAQ.

One detail many guides skip. OpenAI may adjust your headline, description, image or landing page link to suit the conversation. It calls these creative enhancements.

Who sees ChatGPT ads in India?

In India, ads launched for logged-in adults on the Free and Go plans. Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise and Edu accounts do not see ads. OpenAI also excludes accounts it identifies as belonging to users under 18.

When Did ChatGPT Ads Launch in India?

ChatGPT Ads went live in India on 27 August 2026. Self-serve buying opened a few days later.

DateWhat happened?
21 Oct 2026Worldwide, ChatGPT Ads reached 89 countries and over 100,000 advertisers
31 Aug 2026OpenAI opened self-serve Ads Manager in India, ahead of the 4 September date first announced.
27 Aug 2026Ads went live for Free and Go users, with over 50 launch brands, and WPP and Omnicom as the first agency partners.

Many reports still say self-serve opened on 4 September. OpenAI’s own announcement says it launched across India on 31 August. Either way, the Indian ad auction is still young, and it is likely to get busier.

How Much Do ChatGPT Ads Cost in India?

The minimum is ₹725 per campaign per day, according to OpenAI’s campaign guide. What you pay for each click or view is set by an auction, and OpenAI has not published Indian rates.

Your daily budget is an average, not a hard limit. A campaign can spend up to twice the amount on one day. It will never spend more than seven times the amount in a Sunday to Saturday week.

For example, set ₹1,000 a day. One busy day could cost ₹2,000. The full week will not go above ₹7,000.

You choose how to pay when you set your goal:

  • Views. You pay for every 1,000 times your ad is shown (CPM).
  • Clicks. You pay each time someone clicks (CPC).
  • Conversions. The system aims for purchases or leads, and you are billed per view or click.

The default bid strategy, “Maximise Results”, sets bids for you. OpenAI notes it does not guarantee a target cost per lead or sale.

Now the honest part. OpenAI’s only bid guidance is in US dollars. It suggests starting click campaigns at $3 to $5 per click. One Indian agency, UpGrowth, reported about ₹13.67 per click from its own short test. That is one account over a few days, not a market rate. The gap between those two figures shows how uncertain costs still are.

Billing happens after your ads run. A temporary hold may appear on your card when you add it.

Your industry matters more than your budget. OpenAI’s ad policies block several categories outside the US, and some are very common in India.

This hits two sectors that are very common among Kerala businesses: healthcare and real estate. A dental clinic in Kannur cannot run ChatGPT ads today. A builder cannot promote a single apartment project.

There is one small exception for property. A portal can advertise its platform, as long as the ad and landing page do not mention a specific listing.

OpenAI updates these rules often during the beta. Check the policy page before you spend. If your category sits close to these lines, expect a manual review.

Limitations to Know Before You Spend

ChatGPT Ads is still a beta product. These five limits affect Indian businesses the most.

  • Country-level targeting only. You can choose countries and devices (Android, iOS or web), but not a state or city. A Thrissur jeweller cannot show ads only in Thrissur.
  • Custom audiences need scale. To target your own customer list, it must match at least 25,000 users. Most small businesses have far fewer.
  • New accounts stay at home. New self-serve accounts start with home-country targeting. Wider reach may open as the account builds history, so a Kerala business cannot target Gulf customers on day one.
  • No benchmarks yet. OpenAI’s ads FAQ says there are no performance benchmarks across industries. Your first month is partly a learning cost.
  • Your ad may be rewritten. Creative enhancements can change your headline, image and link. Review your live ads every week.

ChatGPT Ads vs Google Ads vs Meta Ads

ChatGPT Ads does not replace Google or Meta. It reaches people at a different moment in their decision.

A simple way to think about it: Meta creates demand, Google catches it, and ChatGPT reaches people while they weigh their options.

Google is also putting ads inside its own AI answers. On 28 October 2026, Google began scaling ads in AI Mode to India. They run through existing Google Ads campaigns, and advertisers cannot opt out of AI Mode separately. That makes Google the closest rival to ChatGPT Ads.

How to Run Your First ChatGPT Ads Test in India

Treat your first campaign as a four-week experiment with a fixed budget and a clear yardstick.

  1. Check that you are allowed. Read OpenAI’s ad policies and confirm your category is not restricted. Make sure your customers are spread across India, not in one town.
  2. Create your account. Sign up at ads.openai.com. Keep your business details ready for verification, plus a payment card.
  3. Set up tracking first. Install the OpenAI Pixel, the Conversions API, or both. Add UTM tags to your links so Google Analytics shows ChatGPT traffic separately.
  4. Start small and focused. Use one campaign with one or two ad groups, each about one product or service. Write context hints that describe real situations. “Parents in Kerala comparing NEET coaching centres” tells the system far more than “NEET coaching”. Add several ad versions with different angles.
  5. Send clicks to a matching page. A NEET coaching ad should open your NEET course page, not your homepage.
  6. Budget for four weeks. At ₹725 to ₹1,000 a day, a month costs roughly ₹22,000 to ₹30,000.
  7. Judge on cost per lead or sale. Compare it with your Google and Meta results for the same offer. Clicks and views alone tell you very little.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Launching before tracking works, then having no way to judge results.
  • Changing bids and ad copy every few days before the system has learned.
  • Expecting city-level results from a channel that only targets countries.

Should Your Business Advertise on ChatGPT?

Yes, if you can test it properly. Not yet, if the rules or your customer base work against you.

Run a test if all of these are true:

  • Your category is allowed under OpenAI’s ad policies.
  • You sell online or serve customers across India.
  • Your website tracking works.
  • Google or Meta ads already bring you profitable leads or sales.

Wait, if any of these apply:

  • You serve one city or district.
  • You are in healthcare, finance, property listings or legal services.
  • Your total monthly ad budget is below about ₹25,000. In our view, a test would take most of it.

Frequently Asked Questions

1. Is there a minimum spend for ChatGPT ads in India?

Yes. Each campaign needs a daily budget of at least ₹725. On a busy day it can spend up to twice that, averaged out over the week.

2. Can I target only Kerala or one city with ChatGPT ads?

No. Campaigns target by country and device, not by state or city. Local businesses usually get better value from Google and Meta.

3. Can dental clinics or hospitals advertise on ChatGPT in India?

Generally no. OpenAI’s ad policies prohibit most health service ads outside the US. Check the policy page for updates.

4. Do ChatGPT ads change ChatGPT’s answers?

No. OpenAI says ads run on separate systems, and advertisers cannot shape or rank its responses.

5. Do I need an agency to run ChatGPT Ads?

No. Any eligible Indian business can sign up at ads.openai.com. An agency helps most with tracking, testing and comparing results across platforms.
