On 24 November 2026, Google marked its November core update as complete. About an hour later, a new notice appeared on the same Google dashboard. It said a December 2026 core update is on the way.

Two core updates this close together is unusual. Your rankings could move twice before the new year. The gap between them also gives you a short window to check your website and get ready.

If you want the background on the first rollout, read our guide on how the November 2026 core update rolled out. This article picks up where that one ends.

The short answer

  • The November 2026 core update ran from 10 to 24 November 2026.
  • Google has announced a December 2026 core update. It is not live yet and has no start date.
  • Google says it will improve how Search handles longer, more complex questions and personalised results.
  • For now, wait one full week, check your data, fix weak pages and avoid big, rushed changes.

What Google Has Confirmed About Both Updates

Google has confirmed the dates of the November update and the purpose of the December one. It has not said when December will start or how long it will run.

In Google’s words, the December update makes “multiple improvements to how our systems understand and rank relevant content.” November was Google’s third core update of 2026. The March and May updates each took about 12 days.

The times above are converted to Indian time from the US times on the Google Search Status Dashboard.

Is the December Core Update Live Yet

No. The December 2026 core update has not started.

Some people are confused because the Search Status Dashboard shows it as “active”. A Google spokesperson told Search Engine Roundtable that the update is neither live nor active. Google will announce the rollout on the day it begins, as it always does.

So if your rankings move this week, December is not the cause. Look at other reasons first. These include the last effects of November, seasonal demand or a technical problem on your site.

What Complex Queries and Personalisation Mean for Your Business

Google has named two areas for the December update. Here is what each one means in plain words. The examples below are our interpretation, not Google’s.

Longer and more complex questions

A complex query is a search that packs several needs into one question. Compare these two searches.

  • “dentist Kannur”
  • “Child-friendly dentist in Kannur for braces, open on Sunday”

That first search is simple. The second asks about the patient, the treatment, the place and the opening day. A clinic page that only says “We offer dental services in Kannur” answers the first search. It does not answer the second.

Pages that cover specific situations have a better chance here. A builder in Kochi could explain what a 3 BHK villa with interior work costs and how long it takes. A school in Calicut could put admission age, fee details and bus routes on one clear page.

Personalised results

Personalisation means Google can adjust results for a person when personalised results are turned on in their Google account. It may use things like their past searches and activity.

This has a practical side effect. The ranking you see on your own phone may not match what a customer sees. That has always been partly true, and the December update may make it more noticeable.

So judge your rankings with Google Search Console, not by searching your business name yourself. Search Console shows average positions across real searches by real people.

Why Google Now Announces Updates in Advance

December is reported to be the first core update Google has announced before its rollout. Google has also published new documentation that explains its approach.

In a new ranking section of Google Search Central, Google says it will give advance notice of significant ranking changes on the Search Status Dashboard. It also says it gives advance notice where UK users may see a significant ranking shift. That follows fair ranking rules set by the UK Competition and Markets Authority (CMA).

According to Search Engine Journal, the CMA rules ask for at least 30 business days’ notice for changes that site owners can act on. Other significant ranking changes need at least 15 business days. These rules take effect on 17 December 2026. Google has not directly said the CMA is the reason it announced December early. That link is an industry view.

The announcements are global, so Indian businesses benefit too. You now get a warning before big changes. A simple habit helps here. Check the Search Status Dashboard once a week, or add its RSS feed to a feed reader.

What Website Owners Noticed During the November Update

These are reports from site owners and SEO professionals, collected by Search Engine Roundtable. Google has not confirmed any of them.

  • Mixed results. Some sites gained over the first weekend. Others reported drops in traffic or sales.
  • Information pages above sales pages. In health and money topics, some owners saw guides rank above commercial pages.
  • Category pages swapped out. Some shop category pages were replaced by articles or single products from the same site.
  • Mass AI content dropped. SEO consultant Glenn Gabe said sites that published AI content at scale fell heavily.
  • Quieter than usual. Overall movement looked smaller than a typical core update, though tracking tools may not have caught everything.

For a local business, the lesson is simple. If your blog answers customer questions better than your service page, Google may show the blog instead. Make sure your main service pages answer real questions too.

Your Plan for the Weeks Between the Two Updates

You have a short gap before December begins. Use it to measure, fix and prepare.

Key dates to note

DateWhat happens
24 Nov 2026The November update finishes.
25 Nov to 1 Dec 2026First full week after November. Use it as your comparison week.
From 2 Dec 2026Compare that week with 3 to 9 November in Search Console.
17 Dec 2026UK CMA notice rules take effect.
December 2026, date not announcedThe December core update begins.

Black Friday falls on 27 November. If you run offers that week, your traffic may change for reasons that have nothing to do with Google.

What to do now

  1. Save your numbers. Export Search Console clicks, impressions and top pages for 3 to 9 November and 25 November to 1 December. Once December starts, these numbers get harder to read.
  2. Rule out technical problems. Check that key pages load, contact forms work and nothing important is blocked from Google.
  3. Improve the pages that bring enquiries. Add answers to the longer questions customers ask you on calls and in WhatsApp.
  4. Show who is behind the content. Add the author’s name, their qualifications and real photos of your work.
  5. Update your Google Business Profile. Add your December and New Year opening hours.

What to hold off on

Avoid redesigns, deleting many pages or changing URLs right now. Suppose you make a big change and December rolls out a week later. You will not know which one moved your rankings. Our November guide lists more mistakes to avoid during a core update.

How to Tell Google About Ranking Changes

Google now invites verified site owners to send feedback about announced ranking updates through Search Console. Google’s ranking documentation explains the steps.

  1. Sign in to Search Console for your verified website.
  2. Use the feedback option and tag your message with the update name, for example, #NovemberCoreUpdate2026.
  3. Include a screenshot of your performance report and the search terms and pages that changed.

Keep your expectations realistic. Google says it reviews feedback as a whole. It does not change rankings for individual pages because of it.

Do Not Mix This Up With the December 2025 Update

Google also ran a December core update last year, from 11 to 29 December 2025. Many articles online about “the December core update” describe that one. Check the year before you act on any advice you read.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the December 2026 core update live?

No. Google announced it on 24 November 2026, but it has not started. Google will post on the Search Status Dashboard on the day it begins.

When will the December 2026 core update start?

Google has only said December 2026. It has not given a start date or said how long the rollout will take.

Has the November 2026 core update finished?

Yes. It ran from 10 to 24 November 2026, which is about 14 days.

Should I change my website before the December update?

Fix clear problems and improve weak pages. Avoid redesigns or mass deletions, because they make it hard to tell what caused a ranking change.

How do I give Google feedback about a core update?

Use the feedback option in Search Console and tag it with the update name, such as #NovemberCoreUpdate2026. Google reviews feedback in bulk and does not adjust individual pages.

What to Do Next

Two core updates in a few weeks can feel worrying, but the steps are simple. Measure first, fix what is clearly weak and give Google time.
